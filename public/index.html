E-commerce Services On Top Platform

Shopify

Amazon

Maximize sales velocity and visibility on Amazon. We handle full Seller Central account setup, Brand Registry, A+ Content design, keyword-optimized product listings, inventory management, and targeted Amazon PPC ad campaign strategies.

Flipkart

Establish a strong marketplace presence on Flipkart. Our services cover seller registration, catalog approval, bulk listing uploads, Flipkart Smart fulfillment support, price optimization, and promotional account management.

Meesho

Scale your reseller and direct-to-consumer reach on Meesho. We offer product cataloging, competitive pricing strategies, order fulfillment coordination, and targeted promotion setups to maximize order volume across regional markets.

Myntra

Position your fashion and lifestyle brand on Myntra. We assist with brand onboarding, high-fashion catalog curation, inventory synchronization, banner promotions, and strict adherence to marketplace SLA and quality standards.

Blinkit

Expand into quick-commerce distribution via Blinkit. We provide vendor onboarding assistance, dark-store inventory planning, optimized product indexing for micro-locations, and real-time stock monitoring.

Snapdeal

Tap into value-conscious shoppers across Tier-2 and Tier-3 markets on Snapdeal. Services include seller onboarding, catalog mapping, order processing setup, and targeted category discount participation.

JioMart

Leverage Reliance's extensive retail network with JioMart integration. We manage merchant seller onboarding, local store inventory syncing, category listing management, and promotional campaigns.

Zepto

Streamline multi-channel distribution across premium quick commerce on Zepto. We oversee catalog compliance, dark store fulfillment workflows, inventory management, and brand store creation.

Meta Ads

Scale your online presence with targeted digital marketing on Facebook and Instagram. We offer complete campaign strategies, Meta Pixel tracking, custom retargeting funnels, and high-converting ad creative management.

Google Ads
